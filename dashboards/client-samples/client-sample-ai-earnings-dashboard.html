Research conclusions

Strongest reported performanceMicrosoftOnly EPS beat of the three. 19% revenue growth at a ~50% operating margin.
Strongest cloud & AI growthAlphabetGoogle Cloud +103% to $30.7B, ahead of Azure +48% and AWS +39%.
Key investment riskCash burn$173.9B combined capex in one quarter. Amazon TTM free cash flow −$42.4B; Alphabet Q3 free cash flow −$9.1B.
Vs expectations: EPS1 beat · 2 missesMicrosoft beat. Amazon missed by ~4%; Alphabet by ~30%, partly from an $8.2B other expense.
Vs expectations: cloudAzure above guideAzure 48% vs 44–45% guided. AWS 39% was below the 40%+ some investors hoped for.
What the market rewardedProfitable growthThe stock that matched growth with margin held up. Fast growth funded by negative cash flow was sold.
Educational takeaway · a repeatable process Writing expectations down before results, then comparing each reported figure with that estimate, shows why a stock moves. Growth alone does not explain it: Alphabet grew cloud 103% and still fell the most.
