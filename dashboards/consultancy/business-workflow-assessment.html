06

Risks & human oversight

AI assistance improves speed and consistency only when controls are designed in from the start.

Important risks

  1. !
    Data protectionClient personal data must be handled in line with UK GDPR, with clear rules on which tools may process it.
  2. !
    Accuracy of AI draftsDrafts can be wrong or incomplete. Nothing reaches a client or a report without review.
  3. !
    Poor source dataAutomating messy spreadsheets spreads errors faster. Data clean-up comes first.
  4. !
    Staff adoptionNew steps fail if the team is not trained and involved in the design.
  5. !
    Over-automationRelationship and judgement tasks lose value if removed from people.

Human oversight requirements

  1. ✓
    Approve before sendingEvery client email, fee statement or document request is reviewed by a named person.
  2. ✓
    Compliance stays manualIdentity, anti-money-laundering and engagement-letter decisions remain with qualified staff.
  3. ✓
    Managers sign off reportsFigures are checked against source before circulation.
  4. ✓
    Clear ownershipEach workflow has an owner who reviews quality monthly.
  5. ✓
    Measured against baselineHours and error rates are tracked so benefits are evidenced, not assumed.
